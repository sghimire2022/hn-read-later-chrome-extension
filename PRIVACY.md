# Privacy Policy — HN Read Later

_Last updated: August 2, 2026_

HN Read Later is a browser extension that lets you save Hacker News stories to a
personal read-later list.

## Data collection

This extension does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any user data.

When you click "read later" on a story, the story's title, URL, and read/unread
status are saved using your browser's local extension storage
(`chrome.storage.local`). This data:

- stays entirely on your own device,
- is never sent to the developer or any third party,
- is deleted when you remove items from your list or uninstall the extension.

The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics, and contains no
tracking of any kind.

## Permissions

- **storage** — used solely to save your reading list locally in your browser.
- **news.ycombinator.com** — the extension runs only on Hacker News, to add the
  "read later" links next to stories. It does not read or store anything else
  from the page.

## Affiliation

This extension is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by,
or sponsored by Y Combinator or Hacker News.
